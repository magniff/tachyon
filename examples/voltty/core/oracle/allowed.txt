# Deliberate differences between voltty and the oracle (alacritty_terminal
# 0.26.0), each with its reason. tests/voltty/run.sh's generator does not
# produce them; a reduced case that shows one is checked against this list.

width  Unicode version. voltty measures with std::unicode::char_width
       (Unicode 18 tables); alacritty_terminal with unicode-width 0.2.2
       (Unicode 16). Code points assigned or re-classified between the two
       (Hebrew U+05C8-05C9, Oriya U+0B53-0B54, U+1ADE-1AF0, Tangut
       extensions, ...: `voltty-oracle width-table` against std's table)
       may take other widths. Width 3 (std, U+2E3B) is taken as 2.

osc    OSC strings longer than 1 MiB are dropped (vte keeps them whole):
       the parser allocates its buffer once and never in `advance`.
